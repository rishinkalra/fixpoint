Questions

How many square feet are in 1 bigha?

It depends on the state: 27,000 ft² in Uttar Pradesh and Haryana, 27,220 ft² in Bihar, 17,424 ft² in Gujarat, 14,400 ft² in West Bengal and Assam, 9,070 ft² in Punjab and 6,804 ft² in Uttarakhand (some sources use 12,000). Values can also differ between districts, so check locally.

How many guntha make an acre?

40 guntha make 1 acre. One guntha is 1,089 ft² (about 101.2 m²), used in Maharashtra, Karnataka and Telangana.

How many marla are in a kanal?

20 marla make 1 kanal: 1 marla is 272.25 ft² and 1 kanal is 5,445 ft². 8 kanal make 1 acre.

How many cents are in an acre?

100 cents make 1 acre. One cent is 435.6 ft² (about 40.5 m²), used in Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh.

How many acres are in a hectare?

1 hectare is 2.471 acres, or 10,000 m².
