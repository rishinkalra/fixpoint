How big is 1 biswa? It depends on the state

The biswa is not one fixed size. It runs from 1,350 to 1,361.25 square feet depending on the state, so always ask which state's biswa is meant.

Questions

How many square feet are in 1 biswa?

It depends on the state: 1,350 sq ft in Uttar Pradesh; 1,361.25 sq ft in Rajasthan.

How many biswa make 1 acre?

32.27 in Uttar Pradesh; 32 in Rajasthan.

Why is the biswa different in each state?

The biswa is a traditional unit that was never fixed nationally, so each region kept its own size. The hectare is now the standard unit, but local units are still used in everyday speech, in sales and in some land records.
