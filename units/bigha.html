How big is 1 bigha? It depends on the state

The bigha is not one fixed size. It runs from 6,804 to 27,225 square feet depending on the state, so always ask which state's bigha is meant.

Questions

How many square feet are in 1 bigha?

It depends on the state: 27,000 sq ft in Uttar Pradesh; 27,220 sq ft in Bihar; 27,000 sq ft in Haryana; 9,070 sq ft in Punjab; 6,804 sq ft in Uttarakhand; 27,225 sq ft in Rajasthan; 17,424 sq ft in Rajasthan (kaccha); 12,000 sq ft in Madhya Pradesh; 17,424 sq ft in Gujarat; 14,400 sq ft in West Bengal; 14,400 sq ft in Assam.

How many bigha make 1 acre?

1.61 in Uttar Pradesh; 1.6 in Bihar; 1.61 in Haryana; 4.8 in Punjab; 6.4 in Uttarakhand; 1.6 in Rajasthan; 2.5 in Rajasthan (kaccha); 3.63 in Madhya Pradesh; 2.5 in Gujarat; 3.02 in West Bengal; 3.02 in Assam.

Why is the bigha different in each state?

The bigha is a traditional unit that was never fixed nationally, so each region kept its own size. The hectare is now the standard unit, but local units are still used in everyday speech, in sales and in some land records.
