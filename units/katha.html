How big is 1 katha? It depends on the state

The katha is not one fixed size. It runs from 720 to 2,880 square feet depending on the state, so always ask which state's katha is meant.

Questions

How many square feet are in 1 katha?

It depends on the state: 1,361 sq ft in Bihar; 720 sq ft in West Bengal; 2,880 sq ft in Assam.

How many katha make 1 acre?

32.01 in Bihar; 60.5 in West Bengal; 15.12 in Assam.

Why is the katha different in each state?

The katha is a traditional unit that was never fixed nationally, so each region kept its own size. The hectare is now the standard unit, but local units are still used in everyday speech, in sales and in some land records.
