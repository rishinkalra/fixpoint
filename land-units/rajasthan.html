1 bigha in Rajasthan

In Rajasthan, 1 bigha = 27,225 square feet. That is 2,529.29 square metres, 0.625 acre or 0.2529 hectare. One acre is 1.6 bigha.

Rajasthan has two bighas. Land records use the pucca bigha (165 feet square, 27,225 sq ft); villages often quote the kaccha bigha (132 feet square, 17,424 sq ft). Ask which one is meant.
